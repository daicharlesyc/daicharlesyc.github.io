Hi all!

For my first blog post, I wanted to talk a little bit about why I began to build my own website.

Over the past few weeks, I have been looking for a research internship in AI, and thus I've been skimming through many AI researchers' websites. Some of them were really cool, because they let me learn a lot or get a sense of who the person behind each website is. I think that's one of the reasons why I have decided to make my own, and that's what I hope to offer too!

Another reason is probably that I've been more and more open to the world over the past few years, and this has brought me huge benefits, allowing me to become more mature and make more friends, so I will keep following this principle. I guess that having my own website is part of this process of opening myself to the world, and I feel that I should make one, and see where this goes. Also, as I've mentioned on the home page, becoming more active goes hand in hand with being more open, as it also opens up possibilities, and building my own website certainly makes me take more initiatives, since I get this small space that I have to build and it is up to me to decide what it will be.

A third reason is that I want to organize my thoughts, so that I will be able to look back in ten years to understand where I came from. But it would be too much of a waste if I kept it only for myself! So, here I am.

That's all for now, I may add some thoughts later.

Good luck & have fun, everyone!
