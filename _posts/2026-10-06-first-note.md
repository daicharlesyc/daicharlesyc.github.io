I'm going to talk a little bit about why I wanted to build my own website.

These last weeks, I have been looking for a research internship in AI, and thus I've been skimming through many AI researchers' websites. Some of them were really cool, because they let me learn a lot or get a sense of their personality. I think that's one of the reasons why I have decided to make my own, and probably what I'm going to build here. 

Another reason is probably that I've been more and more open to the world these last years, and it brought me huge benefits, allowing me to become more mature and make more friends, so I will keep following this principle. I guess that having my own website is part of this process of opening myself to the world, and I feel that I should at least try to make one, and see where this goes.

That's all for now, I may add some thoughts later.
